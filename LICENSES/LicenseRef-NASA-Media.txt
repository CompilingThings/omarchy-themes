NASA images and media usage guidelines
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ (read 18 November 2026)

Pictures marked LicenseRef-NASA-Media in this repository were made by NASA. The guidelines say that NASA content
"generally [is] not subject to copyright in the United States" and that "NASA should be acknowledged as the source of
the material". Each picture's credit line, as its NASA page gives it, is printed on the wallpaper and listed in
CREDITS.md and REUSE.toml.

The same page also says, in its own words:
- "NASA content used in a factual manner that does not imply endorsement may be used without needing explicit
  permission."
- "The NASA Insignia, Logotype, identifiers, and imagery are not in the public domain." "The use of the Insignia,
  Logotype and NASA identifiers is protected by law".
- Pictures of an identifiable person: "using the media for commercial purposes may infringe that person's right of
  privacy or publicity".
- "NASA occasionally uses copyright-protected material of third parties with permission on its website." "Those
  images will be marked identified as copyright protected with the name of the copyright holder." Where a picture's
  NASA page names another rights holder under a licence, this repository lists the picture under that licence.

Copyright law outside the United States may differ. Read the page above before any commercial use.
