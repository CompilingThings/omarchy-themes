U.S. Geological Survey: Copyrights and Credits
https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits (read 22 November 2026)

Pictures marked LicenseRef-USGS-Public-Domain in this repository were made by the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), most
of them by its Bee Inventory and Monitoring Lab. The page says that "USGS-authored or produced data and information
are considered to be in the U.S. Public Domain", and asks that "proper credit be given", for example
"Credit: U.S. Geological Survey" or "U.S. Geological Survey/photo by Jane Doe (if the photographer/artist is known)".
Each picture's credit is printed on the wallpaper and listed in CREDITS.md and REUSE.toml.

The same page also says that "not all information, illustrations, or photographs on our site are" in the public
domain: material used "with permission from the copyright holder" is "generally marked as being copyrighted". Every
picture used here is marked on its Wikimedia Commons page as a work of the USGS (template PD-USGov-USGS).

Copyright law outside the United States may differ.
