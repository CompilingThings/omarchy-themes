# Credits

Written by `tools/credits.py` from each theme's `wallpapers.json`; do not edit by hand.

Every wallpaper prints its credit on its label (photos and fractals) or title block (boards). This file lists each one with its author, licence and source. The code, the theme files and the pictures made for this repository are by CompilingThings; see [README.md](README.md#licence).

**Changes made to every photo:** cut to 16:9, scaled to 3840 x 2160, and a label added in the bottom right corner (the preview is the first one, made small). A wallpaper made from a share-alike (SA) photo is shared under that photo's licence; one made from a non-commercial (NC) photo may not be used commercially.
