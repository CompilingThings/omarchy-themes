# Lantern

An Omarchy theme of insect heads under a fluorescence microscope: ants, flies, a moth, a ladybird, a green shield bug,
a soldier beetle and a scorpionfly, glowing on their own against black. No dye is used on all but one: lit with
violet or ultraviolet light, the shell and the compound eyes give back light of their own (autofluorescence), gold
for the shell and green for the eyes. All fourteen are by one photographer, Alexander Klepnev, ten of them taken in the
summer of 2018 on a Lumam R-8 fluorescence microscope and stitched from several frames. The black of the microscope
is the background, the text is warm and pale, the green glow of the eyes is the accent, and the window border turns
from that green into the gold of a glowing shell.

Each wallpaper has a small label in the bottom right corner (bottom right because terminal text keeps to the
left): what it is, one line in plain words, when it was photographed, the photographer and the licence. The heads
were photographed whole on black; each is set on the screen whole, left of centre, on its own black, so no antenna
or bristle is cut off.

Facts on the labels come from each photo's Commons page: the microscope (Lumam R-8), the light (violet light with a
green beam-splitter plate, or ultraviolet light with a blue one), and that it is autofluorescence, the insect's own
glow. 07's page says no stain was used and that multifocal stacking made the whole head sharp; 13 was stained with
acridine orange and lit with ultraviolet light. The date is the one the photo file records. 07's own title is
"Green princess".

The pictures were chosen from the microscope entries of the Russian Science Photo Competitions 2018 and 2019 and the
European Science Photo Competition 2015 on Wikimedia Commons, then looked at one by one at true screen size. Grain at
screen size (`src/grain.py`, Immerkaer sigma at 2560 x 1440): 0.0 to 0.8. Left out after the full-size look: a
blue-lit ant, a green ant from the front and a close-up of an ant's head (soft in parts), a blowfly (mouthparts
soft), a bee and a striped shield bug (glowing dust specks over the black), a moth from the side (half its fur out
of focus), a second stained fly (lint fibres on its face), a bed bug (hazy), a whole soldier beetle (small in a big
black frame; 11 is the same beetle closer), and a mantis and a paper wasp photographed another way (soft, or
stacking smears).

Every wallpaper uses photowall's `fit` (the whole photo on a field of its own edge colour, its edges faded into it);
03 is kept inside the width a 3:2 screen shows, and 07, 08 and 13 are set a little larger.

```
src/build lantern                # the photos (several hundred MB; some are 300-megapixel stitched panoramas)
./install lantern                #   download once into ~/.cache/omarchy-themes/lantern
omarchy theme set lantern
```
