# Fairy Ring

An Omarchy theme of mushrooms and other fungi from forests round the world: fly agarics in Alaska and New Zealand,
mushrooms that glow in the dark in India and Brazil, amethyst deceivers in the Caucasus, a red latticed stinkhorn in
France. The dark brown-black of a forest floor, cream text like a fly agaric's white flecks, the fly agaric's red as
the accent, and the window border fading from that red into the purple of the amethyst deceiver. The terminal
colours come from the photos too: golden jelly fungus yellow, foxfire green, hoarfrost cyan.

(A fairy ring is a ring or arc of mushrooms over a fungus whose threads spread outward underground
([Wikipedia: Fairy ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairy_ring)); the red-and-white fly agaric became
"iconic in fairy tales" ([Wikipedia: Amanita muscaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanita_muscaria)).)

Each wallpaper has a small label in the bottom right corner (bottom right because terminal text keeps to the
left): the fungus, what it shows in plain words, when it was taken, the photographer and the licence.
Four photos with busy grass, twigs or frost under the label (01, 04, 06, 12) get a dark panel behind it.

Facts on the labels come from each photo's Commons page (name, place, date, description, map position, how many
photos were stacked) and: the latticed stinkhorn smells of rotting meat, which draws the flies that spread its spores
([Wikipedia: Clathrus ruber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clathrus_ruber)); Fronton lies north of Toulouse
by 28 km ([Wikipedia: Fronton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fronton,_Haute-Garonne)). "Poisonous: never eat them" and
"even brighter in life" are from the photographers' own descriptions.

Chosen from Wikimedia Commons' featured, quality and valued pictures of fungi, its bioluminescent fungi category,
and searches for fly agarics, amethyst deceivers and the blue *Entoloma hochstetteri* (165 landscape photos at least
4000 pixels wide with a free licence, none non-commercial or no-changes), then looked at one by one and checked at
true screen size. Every photo was uploaded by
its photographer or imported from Flickr or Mushroom Observer with the licence checked (FlickreviewR, Flickypedia
or a Commons licence reviewer). Grain at screen size (`src/grain.py`, Immerkaer sigma at 2560 x 1440; the themes
so far looked clean up to about 2.5): Mycena clarkeana 0.5, stinkhorn 0.7, fly agarics (Alaska) 0.9, club fungus
1.1, amethyst deceivers 1.6, glowing (Brazil) 2.2, frosted 2.4, fly agarics (New Zealand) 2.6, golden jelly 2.8,
yellow spines 3.1, glowing (India) 3.6, stump 4.2. The last five were looked at in true-size crops: the numbers
come from real fine detail (moss, jelly folds, spines, wart texture) and the dark sky of a night photo, not grain.
Left out: the blue *Entoloma hochstetteri* by Lake Matheson (smeared by the camera's noise reduction at full
size), a second golden jelly fungus and a second pair of fly agarics (too close to ones already in).

```
src/build fairy-ring             # the photos (~200 MB) download once into ~/.cache/omarchy-themes/fairy-ring
./install fairy-ring
omarchy theme set fairy-ring
```
