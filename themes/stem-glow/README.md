# Stem Glow

An Omarchy theme of plant stems, stalks and needles cut across and seen under the microscope: butcher's broom,
bracken, rye, a rose, a pine needle, the stalks of banana, red pepper and grape leaves, and peach and mulberry twigs.
Most of them glow: stained with a dye such as acridine orange and lit with blue or ultraviolet light, the walls of the
water pipes and the woody cells shine gold and green on black. The peach, mulberry and grape are lit another way,
in polarised light, where each kind of tissue takes its own colour. The photos are by five photographers from the
science photo competitions on Wikimedia Commons. The dark the slices glow in is the background, the text is warm and
pale, the gold of the butcher's broom core is the accent, and the window border turns from that gold into the glowing
green of the banana stalk.

Each wallpaper has a small label in the bottom right corner (bottom right because terminal text keeps to the
left): what it is, one line in plain words, when it was photographed, the photographer and the licence.

Facts on the labels come from each photo's Commons page: what was cut, how it was stained and lit, the size where
the page gives it (2 mm, 30 micrometres, 111 times) and the date the page gives. The page for 03 does not name the
plant, so its label does not either. The pine needle's page names two different pines (Siberian in the title, Korean
in the text), so the label just says pine.

The pictures were chosen from 19 plant slices in the science photo competitions on Wikimedia Commons, then looked at
one by one at true screen size. Grain at screen size (`src/grain.py`, Immerkaer sigma at 2560 x 1440): 0.1 to 2.3.
Left out: a closer second photo of the rose stem (soft, with bubbles), two goldenrod stems (soft at full size), the
centre of the banana stalk (a crop of the same slice, with a signature), a black currant leaf stalk (looks almost
the same as the grape), a plum twig (grainier, and close to the peach and mulberry), and two photos of moss (whole
plants, not slices).

Every wallpaper but the bracken and the rose uses photowall's `fit`: the whole slice, centred left of the label, on
the dark of its own photo with a faded edge. The peach and mulberry twigs were photographed on bright blue; they are
set on dark navy (#04071C) instead, and the UV stem on its own dark blue, each with the fade following an oval round
the slice (photowall's `oval`), so the blue stays only as a glow round the slice. Small scale bars on the blue
background of the peach and mulberry photos are painted over with it.

```
src/build stem-glow              # the photos (~160 MB) download once into ~/.cache/omarchy-themes/stem-glow
./install stem-glow
omarchy theme set stem-glow
```
